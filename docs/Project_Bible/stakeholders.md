# Stakeholders

The key stakeholders of the DriveVitals system include:

- **Individual Vehicle Owners:** For monitoring personal driving behavior, vehicle health, and fuel efficiency.
- **Ride-Hailing Drivers:** For optimizing driving patterns and improving operational efficiency.
- **Fleet Operators:** For managing multiple vehicles, tracking driver performance, and reducing operational costs.
- **Logistics Companies:** For monitoring fleet health and ensuring efficient transportation operations.
- **Automotive Workshops:** For assisting in diagnostic analysis and preventive maintenance insights.
- **System Developers & Researchers:** For extending the platform with machine learning and advanced analytics capabilities.
