# Vision

The vision of DriveVitals is to develop an intelligent automotive analytics platform that transforms raw vehicle telemetry into actionable insights for individual drivers, ride-hailing operators, fleet managers, and automotive service providers.

By acquiring real-time telemetry data from a vehicle’s Electronic Control Unit (ECU) through the standardized OBD-II interface, DriveVitals continuously monitors key parameters such as engine RPM, vehicle speed, throttle position, engine load, coolant temperature, Mass Air Flow (MAF), air-fuel ratio (AFR), oxygen sensor readings, fuel-related metrics, and other diagnostic signals supported by the vehicle.

Instead of merely displaying raw sensor values, the system interprets this data using structured engineering logic and rule-based analytics to derive meaningful insights related to vehicle health, driving behavior, fuel efficiency, engine stress, and operational performance.

In its initial phase, DriveVitals focuses on a rule-based analytics approach to identify unsafe driving patterns, inefficient driving behavior, and early indicators of potential mechanical issues. These insights are presented through a real-time web-based dashboard designed for fleet-level monitoring and decision-making.

As the system evolves, machine learning models will be integrated to enhance behavioral analysis, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, fuel efficiency optimization, and personalized driving insights based on historical telemetry data.

Ultimately, DriveVitals aims to contribute to safer roads, reduced operational costs, improved fleet efficiency, and data-driven automotive decision-making by bridging the gap between raw vehicle data and intelligent mobility analytics.
