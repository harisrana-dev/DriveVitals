# Risks

The development and deployment of DriveVitals involve the following risks:

- Inconsistent OBD-II data availability across different vehicle models.
- Hardware limitations or failures in low-quality ELM327 adapters.
- Data loss due to unstable Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connections.
- Incorrect readings due to ECU reporting delays or sensor inaccuracies.
- Limited scalability of rule-based analytics in complex driving scenarios.
- Potential overfitting or lack of training data for future ML components.
- Real-time processing delays under high telemetry load conditions.
